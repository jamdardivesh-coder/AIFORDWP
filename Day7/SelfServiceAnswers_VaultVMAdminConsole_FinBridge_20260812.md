# Self-Service Answers – Top 3 Issues (Fin Bridge Win 11 Migration)
**Date:** 2026-08-12

---

## Issue 1: Cannot Access the Credentials Vault

Open your web browser and go to the credentials vault using the link your team normally uses. Before logging in, clear your browser's saved data — press **Ctrl + Shift + Delete**, select "All time", tick cookies and cached files, then click Clear. Close the browser fully and try again.

If that does not work, try a different browser (for example, if you normally use Edge, try Chrome or vice versa).

**Still not working? Contact the Service Desk and tell them:**
- Your name and team
- The exact error message or what the screen shows
- How many days you have been affected
- Whether anyone on your team can access it

---

## Issue 2: Locked Out of the Admin Console

Wait 15 minutes, then try signing in again — repeated failed attempts can trigger a temporary lock that clears on its own. Make sure you are connected to the VPN before trying.

If a colleague can access the console, ask them to check whether your account appears locked in the user list.

**Still not working? Contact the Service Desk and tell them:**
- Your username and which admin console you are trying to reach
- The exact error or message shown on screen
- Whether this started after the Win 11 update
- Whether other colleagues on your team are affected too

---

## Issue 3: Cannot Connect to Test VMs

First, check your VPN is connected — look for the VPN icon in your taskbar and confirm it shows as active. Then close the remote connection tool, wait 30 seconds, and try connecting to the VM again.

If the VM name or address has changed since the update, ask your team lead whether new connection details have been issued.

**Still not working? Contact the Service Desk and tell them:**
- Your name and the name or address of the VM you are trying to reach
- The error message shown when the connection fails
- Confirmation that your VPN is connected
- How many days this has been happening
