Subject: Update: Sign-in and account lockout issue this morning

We are aware of a sign-in/account lockout issue affecting a growing number of users this morning. Our IT team is actively working on it now.

While we investigate, please try this interim step:
1) If your account is locked, wait 15 minutes, then try signing in again with your usual password.
2) If you recently changed your password on another device, fully restart this device and sign in with the newest password.
3) If sign-in still fails, use the self-service password reset page, then try signing in again.

If you still cannot sign in after one retry, please contact the Service Desk and share: your username, exact time it failed, the message shown on screen, and whether this happened before this week.

We will post the next update by 12:00 PM today, even if we do not yet have full resolution.

Thank you for your patience and professionalism. We understand this is disruptive and are treating it as a priority.
