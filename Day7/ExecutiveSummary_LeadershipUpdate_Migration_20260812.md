The migration is landing well for most employees, and the overall experience has improved week over week. Early disruption around sign-in lockouts, missing cloud files, slow logins, and remote access instability has largely settled, and feedback now reflects a smoother day-to-day experience. Many users are reporting that core tools are working reliably again and that performance feels better than during the initial rollout period.

A small set of issues remains open and is being actively managed. The floor 3 printer problem continues and is now the most visible unresolved pain point for affected teams, so escalation and service restoration are in progress. A new finance-impacting issue has also emerged where large spreadsheet work is unstable, and that is being prioritized with targeted checks and workaround guidance. In parallel, we are reviewing a recent security settings update that appears linked to slower startup experience for one business group, with fast validation steps underway to isolate the exact contributor and apply a safe correction. We will continue structured user updates while these items are driven to closure.
