# Fin Bridge Staff Feedback – Win 11 Migration: Theme Analysis
**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Analyst:** DWP Endpoint Team  
**Source:** 15 post-migration feedback comments from Fin Bridge staff

---

## Theme 1: Credentials Vault Inaccessibility
**Description:** Staff are unable to access the shared credentials vault following the migration.  
**Count:** 3  
**Quotes:**
- *"Shared credentials vault is completely inaccessible, whole team blocked."*
- *"Third day now I can't access the credentials vault, this is urgent."*

**Severity:** Blocker

**Quotes:**

- *"My test VM access is still down, can't do my job today either."*

**Severity:** Blocker
