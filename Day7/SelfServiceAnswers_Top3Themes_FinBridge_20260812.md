If your account is locked, wait 15 minutes, then try signing in again with your usual password. If you recently changed your password on another device, fully restart this device and sign in with the newest password.

If you still cannot sign in after one retry, contact the Service Desk right away. Share: your username, exact time it failed, the message shown on screen, and whether this happened before this week.

Open OneDrive from the taskbar cloud icon and check if you are signed in to your work account. Then open OneDrive on the web (Office portal) and search for the missing file name to confirm whether it is there.

If files are still missing, contact the Service Desk immediately. Share: file names or folder path, last time you saw the files, whether they appear on web OneDrive, and any error message from the cloud icon.

If access is still denied, contact the Service Desk. Share: your username, exact shared folder path, screenshot or text of the error, and whether a teammate next to you can open the same folder.
