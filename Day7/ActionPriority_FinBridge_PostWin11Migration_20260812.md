# Fin Bridge Win 11 Migration – Action Priority & Full Theme Register
**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Analyst:** DWP Endpoint Team  
**Basis:** 15 post-migration feedback comments from Fin Bridge staff

---

## Top 3 Themes to Act on Today

Ranking weighs **both severity and volume**. A single Blocker outranks any number of Minor comments. Where severity is equal, volume and escalation signals (multi-day persistence, team-wide spread) break the tie.

---

### Rank 1 — Credentials Vault Inaccessibility
**Count:** 3 comments | **Severity:** Blocker

**Why it ranks first:**  
All three comments are Blockers. The issue has persisted for at least three days, has spread to the whole team, and has already been escalated to management — the combination of severity, duration, and organisational escalation makes this the highest-priority item regardless of raw count.

**Manager summary:**  
The shared credentials vault has been completely inaccessible to the Fin Bridge team for three days and has been formally escalated — this needs an immediate fix or a workaround before the start of business.

---

### Rank 2 — Admin Console Lockout
**Count:** 2 comments | **Severity:** Blocker

**Why it ranks second:**  
Two Blocker comments, but the language signals a spreading incident — it started with one engineer and has now moved to "the whole team." Active propagation makes this more urgent than a stable two-person issue and elevates it above the VM failures.

**Manager summary:**  
Admin console lockouts that started with one engineer are now affecting the whole Fin Bridge team, suggesting a permissions or policy regression that is actively widening.

---

### Rank 3 — Test VM Access Failure
**Count:** 2 comments | **Severity:** Blocker

**Why it ranks third:**  
Two Blocker comments from what appears to be the same individual on consecutive days. Fully blocking for those affected, but the scope appears contained to a specific role/user rather than spreading team-wide, placing it below the admin console issue.

**Manager summary:**  
At least one engineer has been unable to remote into test VMs for two consecutive days since the migration, completely blocking their work.
