# Help With Black Screen After Sign-In (Quick Steps)
Version: v 1.0  
Date: 07/08/2026  
Status: Draft

If your screen stays black after you sign in, your files are safe. This is usually a short-term problem and can often be fixed by trying again once.

## What you can do now
1. Wait 60 seconds after you sign in to see if your main work screen appears.
2. If nothing appears, sign out.
3. Wait 2 minutes.
4. Sign in one more time.
5. If the screen is still black, stop trying and contact the IT support team.

## Important
- Do not try to sign in many times in a row.
- Trying many times can slow down the fix.
- Your work files are not deleted by this problem.

## Contact IT support if...
Contact IT support if the black screen appears again after one more try, if you cannot sign in at all, or if the problem returns later the same day.

## Details to include in your support request
Please include these details so the team can help faster:
- Your full name and work sign-in name
- Best contact number
- Time the problem started
- Exact message you saw (if any)
- Computer name (from the company sticker or computer settings)
- Where you were working (office or home)
- Whether one more try worked or failed
- A picture of the screen if possible

Thank you for your patience. IT support can usually restore access quickly once they have the details above.
