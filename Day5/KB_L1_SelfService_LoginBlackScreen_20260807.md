# Login Help: Black Screen After Login (Self-Help)
Version: v 1.0  
Date: 07/08/2026  
Status: Draft

If your screen stays black after you log in, your files and data are safe. This problem is usually temporary and can often be fixed with one simple try again.

## What you can do now
1. Wait 60 seconds after login to see if your desktop appears.
2. If nothing appears, sign out of your work screen.
3. Wait 2 minutes.
4. Sign in again once.
5. If the screen is still black, stop trying again and contact the IT Help Desk.

## Important
- Do not keep trying many times in a row.
- Repeated attempts can delay the fix.
- Your work files are not deleted by this issue.

## Contact the IT Help Desk if...
Contact the IT Help Desk if the black screen appears again after one try again, if you cannot log in at all, or if the problem returns later the same day.

## Details to include in your ticket
Please include these details in your support request so the team can help faster:
- Your full name and work username
- Best contact number
- Time the issue started
- Exact message you saw (if any)
- Computer name (from the company sticker or computer settings)
- Where you were working (office or home)
- Whether one try again worked or failed
- Screenshot/photo of the screen if possible

Thank you for your patience. Support teams can usually restore access quickly once they have the details above.
