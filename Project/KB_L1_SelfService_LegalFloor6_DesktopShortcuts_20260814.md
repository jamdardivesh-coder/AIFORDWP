# Help if Your Desktop Shortcuts Go Missing on Floor 6

## Version Header
- Version: v1.0
- Date: 2026-08-14
- Status: Draft
- Audience: Floor 6 end users
- Source: Runbook_LegalFloor6_DesktopShortcuts_Remediation_20260814.md

If your desktop shortcuts disappear, this is usually a restoreable setup issue, not a loss of your files.

1. Check whether the missing shortcuts are only hidden by refreshing the desktop or signing out and back in once.
Expected result: If it was a temporary display issue, the shortcuts return.

2. If the shortcuts are still missing, contact the Service Desk and report your device name, the time you noticed the issue, and which shortcuts are missing.
Expected result: Support has enough detail to match your issue to the known Floor 6 shortcut fix.

3. Do not manually reinstall apps or create many replacement shortcuts unless support asks you to.
Expected result: The support team can restore the correct standard shortcuts cleanly.

Verification:
The issue is fixed when the expected shortcuts are back on your desktop and they open the correct apps.

Rollback:
There is no user rollback step. If the wrong shortcuts appear or the restored shortcuts do not work, report it to the Service Desk immediately.
