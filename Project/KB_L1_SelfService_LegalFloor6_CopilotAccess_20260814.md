# Help if Copilot Shows a Legal Matter You Do Not Expect to See

## Version Header
- Version: v1.0
- Date: 2026-08-14
- Status: Draft
- Audience: Floor 6 end users
- Source: Runbook_LegalFloor6_CopilotAccess_Remediation_20260814.md

If Copilot shows a client matter or document you do not believe you should see, stop and report it right away.

1. Do not open, share, download, or act on the content.
Expected result: The issue is contained and no extra exposure is created.

2. Take a screenshot of what Copilot showed, including the time if possible.
Expected result: Support has evidence to investigate without asking you to reproduce it.

3. Contact the Service Desk and provide your name, device name, the time you saw it, and the matter or document name.
Expected result: A security investigation ticket can be opened quickly.

4. Do not keep retrying the same prompt.
Expected result: Audit evidence stays clean for the investigation team.

Verification:
The issue is considered fixed only after Security/Data-Governance confirms the incorrect access path was removed and support tells you validation is complete.

Rollback:
There is no user-performed rollback. If your normal work access changes unexpectedly after the fix, report that to the Service Desk immediately.
