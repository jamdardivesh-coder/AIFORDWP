# Escalation Email: Potential Unauthorized Matter Exposure via Microsoft 365 Copilot

## Subject Line
Potential Unauthorized Matter Exposure via Microsoft 365 Copilot - Immediate Security and Governance Investigation Required

## Executive Summary
A user reported that Microsoft 365 Copilot surfaced a matter she states she was never authorized to access. Because Copilot generally respects existing Microsoft 365 permissions, this indicates a potential underlying access-control or data-governance exposure requiring immediate cross-functional investigation.

## Escalation Statement (Two Sentences)
We are escalating this as a potential unauthorized data access incident pending validation of effective permissions across SharePoint, Teams, group membership, sharing links, and Microsoft Graph-visible access paths. Security Operations, Microsoft 365 Engineering, and Information Governance support is required immediately to determine scope, contain risk if confirmed, and preserve audit integrity.

## Suggested Recipients
- Security Operations
- Microsoft 365 Engineering
- Information Governance
- Identity and Access Management
- Legal/Privacy (as required by data sensitivity)
