# Legal Floor 6 Incident Update for Partners

**Date:** August 14, 2026

---

## What Happened

On Monday morning, staff on Legal Floor 6 experienced three workplace problems that occurred shortly after a new document management software was installed on Friday afternoon.

**Issue 1 – Computers would not start up or took very long to log in**
At least a dozen Floor 6 users reported they could not log into their computers, or the login process was extremely slow. This affected daily work access.

**Issue 2 – Missing desktop shortcuts**
One or more users found that shortcuts on their computer desktops had disappeared, making certain programs harder to access.

**Issue 3 – Unexpected access to a client matter in Copilot**
A paralegal on Floor 6 discovered that Copilot (a workplace research assistant) showed them a client matter they believe they were never supposed to have access to. This raised an immediate data security concern.

---

## What's Being Done

**Login and startup issue (Issue 1)**
The new document management software deployment was identified as the likely cause. The deployment has been reversed, and users' login times have returned to normal. This resolution has been applied and confirmed.

**Missing desktop shortcuts (Issue 2)**
The desktop shortcuts were restored using automatic tools, and users now have them back. This issue is resolved.

**Copilot access issue (Issue 3)**
Our Security and Data Governance team completed a full investigation into why the paralegal saw that client matter. They confirmed their findings and took remediation steps to prevent recurrence. The specifics of their findings and remediation are being finalized (to confirm).
