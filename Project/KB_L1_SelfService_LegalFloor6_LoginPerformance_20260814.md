# Help if Floor 6 Login Is Very Slow or Fails Again

## Version Header
- Version: v1.0
- Date: 2026-08-14
- Status: Draft
- Audience: Floor 6 end users
- Source: Runbook_LegalFloor6_LoginPerformance_Remediation_20260814.md

If sign-in is failing or takes much longer than normal on Floor 6, it may match a known software rollout issue.

1. Wait a short time and try one more sign-in attempt.
Expected result: If the issue was temporary, you get in normally.

2. If sign-in is still failing or extremely slow, contact the Service Desk and provide your device name, your location, and the time the problem started.
Expected result: Support can match your issue to the known Floor 6 login incident pattern.

3. If support asks, sign out and back in after they confirm the fix has been applied.
Expected result: Your device picks up the corrected software assignment state.

Verification:
The issue is fixed when you can sign in normally again without the long delay or failure.

Rollback:
There is no user rollback step. If login remains slow after support confirms the fix, report it immediately so the next investigation path can start.
